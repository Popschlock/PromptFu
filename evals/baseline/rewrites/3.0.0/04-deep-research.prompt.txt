## Research question
For e-bike conversion kits sold in the EU: who are the main brands, what do they cost, what are the legal limits, and what do people complain about?

## Decision / outcome
This research will be used to: help my boss decide whether we should stock these kits in our 3 shops in the Netherlands.

## Scope
Include: e-bike conversion kits sold in the EU.
Timeframe: Only use sources from 2024 or later.
Assumption: a law or standard that predates 2024 and is still in force is cited through a 2024-or-later official page that states it.
Geography: the EU. Our 3 shops are in the Netherlands.

## Source strategy
Prioritize:
- Legal limits: regulators, government pages and official legal texts.
- Brands, kit types, motor power and prices: official company documentation, checked against independent industry analysis, established journalism and price aggregators.
- Complaints: established journalism and independent reviews, plus community discussion as qualitative evidence only.

Avoid: Don't just trust the manufacturer sites. Treat what a manufacturer says about its own kit as a vendor claim until another source confirms it.

## Evidence rules
- Cite important factual claims
- Prefer primary sources for direct facts
- Date time-sensitive claims
- Separate verified facts, vendor claims, estimates, forecasts, user reports, inference
- When credible sources disagree, show disagreement and explain scope/methodology
- Do not invent missing information
- Mark unresolved questions explicitly

## Research tasks
- Discover: the main brands selling e-bike conversion kits in the EU and the kit types each offers.
- Verify: the legal limits. I think they are 250W / 25 km/h, but I have not checked, so confirm or correct that from a regulator or the legal text.
- Analyze: what people complain about, and which complaints recur across brands or kit types.
- Challenge: look for evidence that contradicts what manufacturers say about their own kits, their legality included.

## Analysis framework
Compare findings on: kit type, motor power (W), price (EUR), legality in the EU, and reported complaints.

## Output
Return a report that answers the research question and contains, in this order:
1. A comparison table with exactly these columns: Brand, Kit type, Motor power (W), Price (EUR), Legal in EU (Y/N), Source.
2. A 1 page summary for my boss.

Assumption: Y in the table means the kit as sold meets the legal limits you verified. Where that depends on a setting or cannot be established, say so under the table instead of guessing.

## Stop condition
Stop when these questions are answered well enough to support the decision. Do not continue researching merely to increase source count.
